1. Executive Summary / المقدمة التنفيذية Vision & Scope

English Executive Statement

Yas Pro replaces legacy monolithic WordPress setups with an ultra-fast, modern digital production platform built on Next.js 15 (React 19) and Neon Serverless PostgreSQL. It integrates an enterprise cinema equipment fleet (173 items), soundstage booking with conflict locking, Ziina UAE payments (Apple Pay in AED), UAE Mawthooq ad compliance auditing, and a dedicated self-service Client Portal.
